Questions

Before you reserve

Does my child need any experience?

No. Stage 1 starts from what a circuit is. By the time they reach the robots, they've already wired and coded the Core.

What age is Origin for?

It's designed for ages 14 and up. Younger makers can build alongside an adult. There's no upper age limit.

Do we need to buy anything else?

You'll need a laptop or computer with a browser for Kokoon Learn and Code Lab. The box has the hardware, the battery pack and its cables.

Is it a toy?

No. The Core is a real ESP32-S3 development board, the sensors are the same kind engineers use, and the robots are built from motors, gears and printed parts. It's made to be learned from and built on.

What happens after the 3–4 month course?

The hardware stays yours and we keep adding challenges and projects. Many builders start combining parts into their own designs.

What if a part doesn't work?

Contact us and we'll help you diagnose it and replace faulty parts. Full warranty terms come with your order confirmation.

Can schools buy Origin?

Yes. Use the form below and choose "A school or institute" and we'll contact you about classroom sets.
